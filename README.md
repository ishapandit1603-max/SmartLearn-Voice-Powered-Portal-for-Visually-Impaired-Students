# 🎓 **SmartLearn — Voice-Powered Learning & Student Management System**

> **An inclusive learning platform designed for visually impaired students, combining student management, progress tracking, voice assistance, and regional language support.**

## 📚 **Project Overview**

**SmartLearn** provides a centralized platform to manage **student records, academic progress, and study content** with **voice-powered assistance**. Its regional language capability supports adoption by **local schools and government initiatives**, making digital learning more accessible across different regions.

## ✨ **Key Features**

- 👨‍🎓 **Student Record Management**
- 📈 **Academic Progress Tracking**
- 🎙️ **Voice-Powered Study Assistance**
- 📖 **Accessible Study Content**
- 🗣️ **Regional Language Support**
- ♿ **Visually Impaired Student Support**
- 🏫 **School & Government Initiative Integration**
- 🌍 **Multi-Region Accessibility**

## 🛠️ **Tech Stack**

**Database:** MySQL  
**Programming:** Python  
**Data Management:** Microsoft Excel  
**AI / Language Processing:** NLP

## ⚙️ **Workflow**

**Student Data** 👨‍🎓 → **MySQL Database** 🗄️ → **Progress & Content Management** 📚 → **NLP & Voice Assistance** 🎙️ → **Accessible Learning** ♿

## 🎯 **Objective**

To build an **accessible and inclusive digital learning platform** that enables visually impaired students to access educational content, track their progress, and learn through **voice and regional language support**.
